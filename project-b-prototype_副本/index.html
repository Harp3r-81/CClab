I believe the future can be exhausting. Even as humanity develops technologies that allow us to defy gravity and explore the universe, our inner struggles remain unchanged. Anxiety and intrusive thoughts do not disappear simply because we advance technologically—they may even intensify over time. As time stretches forward and innovation accelerates, it seems unlikely that we will ever fully eliminate these feelings. Instead, what remains constant is our reliance on one another. In the end, perhaps it is other human beings who ground us and help us navigate this overwhelming future.
