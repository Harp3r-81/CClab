documented by Harper Zou

Scientific Name:

Space Moth

Discovered by / at:

Hubble Space Telescope, Between Pluto and its moon, Charon

Family:

Nobody knows. They’re from another world, after all! For *this* specific space moth you’re looking at, her family contains her mom, her dad and her pet.

Habitat:

Space Moths love icey dwarf planets! The cold is both enjoyable for them and can protect them from the sun. Once they're exposed to extreme heat, they will risk the danger of their wings burning up!

Appearance:

Space Moths got the name due to their moth-like appearances! They're usually transparent or white, and only show their colors when they're with people they trust.

Key Characteristics:

  • Its body is really sensitive to heat
  • Likes making friends
  • Likes Icecream
  • Sensitive to sounds

What we know so far about Space Moth?

This creature is really approachable!!! — unless you approach it wrongly. Please remember to say *hi* to it before rudely interrupting its life! When it favors you, it will no longer stay white, instead, it will appear in bright pink to show it likes you!


Guidelines for human interaction with this creature
  • Press “hi” on the keyboard to say hi to it
  • Press the mouse to irritate it (Please think twice)
  • Move the mouse to see how its whiskers move with it.
  • It is more visible when you stay close to it so move the mouse closer to it
